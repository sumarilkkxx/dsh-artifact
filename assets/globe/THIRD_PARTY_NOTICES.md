# ECharts-GL globe resources

The three files in this directory are the exact offline resources used by the
Apache ECharts "ECharts-GL Hello World" globe example:

- `world.topo.bathy.200401.jpg`
- `starfield.jpg`
- `pisa.hdr`

Source: <https://echarts.apache.org/examples/en/editor.html?c=globe-echarts-gl-hello-world&gl=1>

They are kept together solely to make the plugin's ECharts-GL globe baseline
work offline. Preserve this notice and review the upstream provenance before
redistributing the assets independently.
